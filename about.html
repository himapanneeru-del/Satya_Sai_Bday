His Divine Life

Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba

Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba was born on 23 November 1926 in Puttaparthi, Andhra Pradesh. His life was dedicated to love, spirituality, education, healthcare and service to humanity.

His message inspired millions of people around the world to follow the path of truth, righteousness, peace, love and non-violence.

His Legacy

His teachings continue to inspire people to see unity in humanity and serve others with compassion.
