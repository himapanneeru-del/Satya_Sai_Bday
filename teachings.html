Five Human Values

Bhagawan taught humanity five universal values.

❤️ LOVE

Love is the foundation of all values.

🕊️ PEACE

Find peace within yourself.

🌟 TRUTH

Always follow the path of truth.

🙏 RIGHT CONDUCT

Let your actions reflect goodness.

🌸 NON-VIOLENCE

Respect and care for every living being.

"Love All, Serve All"

Om Sai Ram 🙏
